# Code of conduct

This project welcomes everyone who wants to help, whatever their background or experience.

## What is expected

- Be considerate and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and explain why when you decline one yourself.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions and block people who do not follow this code. To report a problem privately, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests and other spaces.
